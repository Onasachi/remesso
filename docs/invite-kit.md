# Inviting real senders

Who counts, for Agents on Open Rails: a wallet that is **not ours, was never
funded by us, and had its own Celo activity before 2026-10-06**. So: no gas
top-ups, no "here's 5 USDT to try it", no wallets we control. Invite people
who already send money home and already hold stablecoins on Celo.

Every link below opens the send form with the amount, currency and rhythm
filled in. The sender still chooses the recipient, reads every field and signs
in their own wallet — a link can prefill, never pay.

> MiniPay: Remesso is not yet a listed Mini App, so a link cannot open it
> inside MiniPay for ordinary users. Until it is listed, invite people who use
> a browser wallet (MetaMask, Valora, Rabby) on Celo.

## Where to find them

- Celo community: Celo Discord / Telegram, Celo Colombia, Celo Brasil, Celo
  Argentina (via Ripio's community), Celo Africa DAO.
- People already sending stablecoins home: crypto groups of Nigerians abroad,
  Argentine and Venezuelan diaspora groups.
- The hackathon's own Telegram (t.me/realworldagentshackathon) — other
  builders are real Celo users with their own history.

## What to tell them honestly

- It's live on Celo mainnet and **unaudited**. Start small.
- The contract only moves the amount they signed, to the person they signed
  for, at the rhythm they signed — and they can pause or cancel any time.
- Remesso charges 0.25% per payment.
